# Joel Kickoff Call — Sales System Cheat Sheet

## 1. Let Joel Lead First
- Let him go through onboarding, expectations, training period, and anything he already prepared.
- Listen for anything that answers the sections below so I don’t repeat questions unnecessarily.
- Main goal by the end of the call: know **who I’m calling, what I’m selling, how I’m calling them, what success looks like, and what happens next.**

---

## 2. Target Market / ICP

### What has already worked?
- What kinds of businesses have been the best fit so far?
- Which industries have responded best?
- Which industries should we avoid?
- What do the best current $48/month clients have in common?

### Website condition
- Are we mainly targeting:
  - no website?
  - outdated/bad website?
  - broken website?
  - Facebook-only page?
  - all of the above?
- Which type tends to convert best?
- Is there a specific website problem that makes a lead especially attractive?

### Business profile
- What size business is ideal?
- Are owner-operated businesses the priority?
- Who is the best decision-maker to reach: owner, manager, office manager, etc.?
- Any business types or company sizes that are clearly a bad fit?

### Geography
- California and Oregon only for now?
- Specific cities/metros first, or statewide?
- Any areas to prioritize or exclude?

### Qualification / disqualification
- What makes a lead obviously worth calling?
- What makes a lead obviously not worth calling?
- Are there any hard filters I should apply before a lead gets added to the list?

### Final ICP check
- Summarize the ICP back to Joel before moving on.

---

## 3. Product / Offer Knowledge

### Core offer
- What exactly does the $48/month include?
- Any setup fee?
- Month-to-month or contract?
- Can clients cancel anytime?
- Does the price ever change depending on the client?

### What happens after someone says yes?
- What is the exact onboarding process?
- What information/assets do we need from the client?
- How long until the website is live?
- Do I book them into a call with Joel, or can they sign up directly?
- Who handles the client after I hand them off?

### Included vs. extra
- What ongoing work is included each month?
- What requests fall outside the $48 plan?
- What makes a project become a higher-ticket/custom project?
- Any limits on pages, edits, revisions, hosting, domains, maintenance, etc.?

### Why people buy
- What do current clients value most?
- Why do people choose this over Wix, Squarespace, Fiverr, or a normal agency?
- What usually gets someone over the line?
- What is the strongest value proposition in Joel’s own words?

### Objections
- What objections come up most often?
- Where do prospects usually lose interest?
- Any promises/claims I should specifically avoid making?
- What kind of customer should absolutely not buy this?

### Proof
- Do we have:
  - testimonials?
  - example websites?
  - before/after examples?
  - case studies?
  - long-term clients I can reference?

---

## 4. Previous Sales Material
- Ask for the existing sales manual.
- Ask for the current/old script.
- Ask for any previous call recordings.
- Ideally get:
  - one good/successful call
  - one average/bad call
- Ask which previous callers performed best and why.
- Ask what objections previous callers heard repeatedly.
- Use the recordings + offer knowledge to build Script V1 afterward.

---

## 5. Lead Scraping Workflow

### My proposal
- Start with a **50-lead test scrape** first.
- Inspect quality manually before scaling.
- If the 50 looks good, scale the same process to the larger target area.

### Confirm what fields Joel wants
Potential fields:
- Company name
- Owner/contact name
- Phone number
- Website
- City / state
- Business category
- Website status/problem
- Any other qualification field Joel cares about

### Questions / checks
- What exact criteria should Codex use to qualify a lead?
- Does Joel care about owner names being present?
- Does he want leads with no website, bad websites, or both?
- Any industries or business types to exclude?
- Does he need email at all?
- How should leads be organized before import into the dialer/CRM?
- After the 50-lead test, review the output together before scaling.

## 7. Expectations / KPIs

### Clarify what success means
- What does Joel want to see in **Week 1**?
- What does he want to see by the end of **Month 1**?
- What would make the first **4 months** successful?
- Which matters most to him:
  - calls made?
  - pickup rate?
  - real conversations?
  - callbacks?
  - booked meetings?
  - closed clients?

### Call expectations
- Confirm the minimum daily call expectation.
- Confirm whether the 40 calls/day is simply a floor or an actual target.
- Are there expected calling hours every day?
- Any days/times he specifically wants prioritized?
- Is there a minimum number of hours expected even if call volume is high?

### Performance review
- How will monthly progress reviews work?
- What numbers will we review?
- What would cause us to change the ICP/script/process?
- At the four-month review, what result does Joel need to justify continuing the role?

---

## 8. Reporting / Communication

### Daily Slack summary
Clarify exactly what Joel wants included.

Possible daily metrics:
- Total calls
- Pickups
- Meaningful conversations
- Not interested
- Callbacks
- Meetings booked
- Qualified opportunities
- Closed clients
- Common objections
- Notes / patterns noticed

### Ask
- How detailed should the daily summary be?
- Does he want a simple message or a structured template?
- Should every call be logged individually?
- Does he want detailed notes only for interested prospects / booked meetings?
- Should the GHL pipeline be the main source of truth?
- Weekly or bi-weekly meeting?
- What should we review during those meetings?
- Where should urgent questions go: Slack DM, channel, email?

---

## 9. Compensation Details

### Base pay
- Confirm **$500/month**.
- Confirm **paid weekly**.
- What exact day of the week will payment be sent?
- What payment method are we using?
- When does the first payment happen?

### Commission
Get this completely unambiguous:
- Is the commission **10%**, **$10**, or something else?
- When does commission start?
- What does “past the minimum” mean exactly?
- Is commission one-time or recurring?
- Does a client have to actually pay before commission counts?
- What happens if the client cancels/refunds?
- When are commissions paid?

### Raises
- What exactly happens every 25 clients?
- Is it a $10 raise, 10% raise, or something else?
- Is the raise permanent?
- Does it apply to base pay, commission, or both?

### Four-month commitment
- Confirm Joel’s four-month employment commitment.
- Confirm the role stays paid during that period as long as agreed work is being done.
- Clarify the target he mentioned for breaking even.
- What specifically happens at the four-month review?
