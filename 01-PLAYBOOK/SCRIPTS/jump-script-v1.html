2. THE PITCH

(One turn, two breaths, then stop talking. The reason line comes from the lead's class in the CSV, checked before you dial.)

(likely fires here → NOT INTERESTED · ALREADY HAVE A WEBSITE · WE'VE GOT OUR FACEBOOK · DON'T NEED A WEBSITE · BURNED BEFORE · NO TIME · HOW MUCH)

  • "Hey [BUSINESS NAME], my name is Kenzo, and I work with JU.MP Web Design. I saw your business on Google Maps and noticed that [REASON], so I figured I'd give you a quick call to offer our services."

    • CLASS A, no website or social-only → [REASON] = "you don't seem to have a website"
    • CLASS A, dead or broken site → [REASON] = "your website doesn't seem to be working"
    • CLASS B, outdated site → [REASON] = "your website looks like it hasn't been updated in a while" → CLASS B FORK, before the offer breath

  • "We partner with small businesses in order to run their websites and help create better online branding. We charge $48 a month, and that covers everything you would need to get your website up and running and maintained. Is that something you would be interested in?"

    (Stop talking. Let them respond.)

    INTERESTED / "YEAH, ACTUALLY" / "WE'VE BEEN MEANING TO DO THAT":

    • → 4. THE BOOK, straight in

    CURIOUS / "TELL ME MORE" / "WHAT DOES THE $48 COVER?":

    • "The $48 covers four main things: your hosting and domain, SSL and security checks, proactive website monitoring, and four edits or revisions every month. So rather than paying someone to build a website once and then having to figure out maintenance yourself, we're managing it for you on an ongoing basis."

      • Warms up → 4. THE BOOK
      • Asks more → 3. HIS QUESTIONS

    "HOW MUCH?" (missed the price):

    • "$48 a month, flat, and there's no upfront cost."

      • → back to the interest question

    NOT THE DECISION MAKER / "YOU'D HAVE TO TALK TO THE OWNER":

    • → GATEKEEPER

DISPOSITIONS (every call ends in exactly one; the note carries where it died)

  • NO ANSWER: nobody picked up, or it rang to voicemail. Recycle at a different hour in two to three days. Never leave a voicemail.
  • VM (RANG OUT): logged only if Teleblast tracks it separately from NO ANSWER; same recycle rule.
  • BAD NUMBER: dead line, wrong business, closed. Fix from the lead source or discard.
  • CALLBACK SET: a named time or window, from him or the gatekeeper. Kept to the minute. The note says where to resume.
  • MEETING BOOKED: consultation set with the web designer, wrap complete, details sent to Joel.
  • NOT INTERESTED: a clear no, anywhere on the map. Cooling window, then the rotation.
  • NOT A FIT: happy with their current setup, chain or franchise, or genuinely excluded. No redial without a real reason.
  • DO NOT CALL: they said the words. Honored instantly and forever.
