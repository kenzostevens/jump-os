# 1. OPEN

- **"Hi, are you guys open right now?"**

  "YES" / "YEAH, WE'RE OPEN" / "YEAH, WHAT DO YOU NEED?" / "NO, WE'RE CLOSED ACTUALLY":

  *(Either answer, roll straight in.)*

  - **"Oh okay. Yeah, so my friend Julie Becker actually gave me your number. She said you guys do really good work, but you guys don't have a website or anything like that. Is that true?"**

    - CLASS A, no website or social-only → the line as written
    - CLASS A, dead or broken site → **"...She said you guys do really good work, but she tried to find your website and couldn't get it to work. Is that true?"**
    - CLASS B, outdated site → **"...She said you guys do really good work, but your website looks like it hasn't been touched in a while. Is that true?"**

    CONFIRMS THE GAP / "YEAH, WE DON'T HAVE ONE" / "YEAH, IT'S BEEN BROKEN":

    - → 2. THE OFFER

    "WHO?" / "I DON'T KNOW A JULIE BECKER":

    - **"She's a friend of my mom's, honestly you probably wouldn't know her. But yeah, she passed your number along."**
      - → 2. THE OFFER, straight in

    "WE ACTUALLY DO HAVE A WEBSITE":

    - **"Oh really? Okay, maybe I just couldn't find it. Where do people usually find you guys?"**
      - *(If a real site exists, note it for the list.)*
      - → ALREADY HAVE A WEBSITE (arsenal)

    "YOU'D HAVE TO TALK TO THE OWNER ABOUT THAT" / IT'S CLEARLY A GATEKEEPER:

    - → GATEKEEPER

    "HOLD ON, LET ME GRAB THE OWNER":

    - Ride it. Owner picks up → run the referral fresh from the top. Never ask for the transfer; only ride one that's offered.

    "WHAT'S THIS ABOUT?" / SUSPICIOUS:

    - → 2. THE OFFER, straight in

  CONFUSED / "SAY THAT AGAIN?":

  - Repeat the opener slowly. Never rephrase.

  WRONG NUMBER / "THINK YOU GOT THE WRONG PLACE" / CLOSED DOWN / NOT THE BUSINESS:

  - **"Oh, got it, my mistake. Have a good one."**
    - → out, BAD NUMBER

*(One breath, then the ask, then stop talking. The referral already named the gap, there is no reason line here.)*

*(likely fires here → WHAT ARE YOU GETTING OUT OF IT · NOT INTERESTED · SOMEONE'S ALREADY ON IT · WORD OF MOUTH · TOO BUSY FOR MORE WORK · HAD A SITE, GOT NOTHING · BURNED BEFORE · HOW MUCH)*

- **"Gotcha. So my name's Kenzo, I'm a business owner like yourself, and at the moment we're actually creating free website designs for local businesses in [AREA] with no strings attached."**

- **"So I was curious if you'd be opposed if we built one out for your business completely free of charge over the next few days, and then we can present it to you over a really quick call once we've built it out, just to see what you think. Does that sound good?"**

  *(Stop talking. Let them respond.)*

  ACCEPTS / "SURE" / "SOUNDS GOOD" / "WHY NOT":

  - → 4. WRAP

  "WHAT ARE YOU GETTING OUT OF IT?" / "WHY FREE?" / "WHAT'S THE CATCH?":

  - **"We're trying to expand our portfolio with simpler websites for small businesses, and once we've got a larger portfolio, we can advertise towards bigger ticket companies. So the design's genuinely free. If you like it and want it launched and managed, that part's $48 a month, and if not, no worries."**
    - Satisfied → back to the ask
    - Asks more → 3. HIS QUESTIONS

  CURIOUS / ASKS A QUESTION:

  - → 3. HIS QUESTIONS

  "JUST SEND IT TO ME AND I'LL SAY YES OR NO":

  - **"Yeah, we can do that. The reason we like doing a really quick call is so you can tell us what to change and we fix it right there. It's like ten, fifteen minutes, and if you don't like it, that's it."**
    - Takes the call → 4. WRAP
    - Still wants it sent → get the email, read it back → **"Alright, we'll get building and send it over. I'll give you a ring [DAY] to hear what you thought."** → CALLBACK SET

  NOT INTERESTED:

# 3. HIS QUESTIONS

*(His questions, never yours. There is no discovery on this call, that's the web designer's meeting. Answer short and end every turn one step toward the wrap.)*

*(likely fires here → HOW MUCH · WIX IS CHEAPER · TEMPLATES · E-COMMERCE · CUSTOM REQUESTS · WHO ARE YOU GUYS)*

  "HOW MUCH?" / "WHAT'S THE CATCH?" / "FREE HOW?":

If you like it and want it launched and managed, it's $48 a month, and that covers your hosting and domain, SSL and security checks, proactive site monitoring, and four edits every month.   - **"No. That's one of the main reasons our service exists. We handle the ongoing website management so you don't have to figure everything out yourself."**

  - **"You get four edits or revisions each month as part of the $48 package."**

  "DO YOU USE TEMPLATES?" / "WHAT MAKES YOU DIFFERENT?":

  - **"One thing that makes us different is that we don't just throw stock images onto a template. We create custom illustrations and branding so the website actually feels like it belongs to your business."**

  "HOW FAST CAN IT BE UP?":

  - **"We'd have the draft built over the next few days, and the designer gives you the exact timeline on the call."** *(Never promise a launch date.)*

  "CAN I SEE YOUR WORK?":

  - **"Yeah. I can send you one of our recent [INDUSTRY] sites right after this call so you can see the style."** *[EXAMPLES: get the example-site links from Joel]*

  "CAN YOU FIX THE SITE I HAVE?" *(Class B)*:

  - **"That's exactly what the free design is for. He'll look at what you've got, build out what he'd do with it, and show you on the call. You don't pay anything unless you like it."**
    - → 4. WRAP

  AFTER ANY ANSWER, THE STEP FORWARD:

  - Warm → **"Tell you what, the easiest way to see if it's for you is just to look at the draft."** → 4. WRAP
  - Another question → answer it, same law
  - Cooling off → NOT INTERESTED (arsenal)

# 4. WRAP *(every booking, every item)*

*(Booking runs on Joel's Calendly, live on the call, set a few days out so the draft exists by then. The call is a phone call from the web designer.)*

*[CONFIRM WITH JOEL: the draft gets built BEFORE the presentation call, per this script's offer. If he'd rather walk them through first and draft after, item 5's line changes.]*

2. **"And what was your name? ...Alright, [NAME]."**
3. **"What's the best number for him to reach you at?"** → read it back

# DISPOSITIONS *(every call ends in exactly one; the note carries where it died)*

- NO ANSWER: nobody picked up, or it rang to voicemail. Recycle at a different hour in two to three days. Never leave a voicemail.
- VM (RANG OUT): logged only if Teleblast tracks it separately from NO ANSWER; same recycle rule.
- BAD NUMBER: dead line, wrong business, closed. Fix from the lead source or discard.
- CALLBACK SET: a named time or window, from him or the gatekeeper. Kept to the minute. The note says where to resume.
- MEETING BOOKED: presentation call set with the web designer, wrap complete, details sent to Joel.
- NOT INTERESTED: a clear no, anywhere on the map. Cooling window, then the rotation.
- NOT A FIT: happy with their current setup, genuinely booked out, chain or franchise, or otherwise excluded. No redial without a real reason.
- DO NOT CALL: they said the words. Honored instantly and forever.
