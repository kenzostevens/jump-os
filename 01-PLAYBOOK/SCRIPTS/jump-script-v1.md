# 1. OPEN

- **"Hey, is this [BUSINESS NAME]?"**

  DIRECT ANSWER / "YES" / "THIS IS SHE" / "SPEAKING" / "YEAH, WHAT CAN I DO FOR YOU?":

  - → 2. THE PITCH

  "WHO'S CALLING?" / "WHAT'S THIS ABOUT?":

  - → 2. THE PITCH *(it opens with your name and the reason, it answers the question)*

  OWNER'S OUT / "CAN I TAKE A MESSAGE?" / "I JUST WORK HERE":

  - → GATEKEEPER

  CONFUSED / "SAY THAT AGAIN?":

  - Repeat the opener slowly. Never rephrase.

  WRONG NUMBER / CLOSED / NOT THE BUSINESS:

  - **"Oh, got it, my mistake. Have a good one."**
    - → out, BAD NUMBER

# 2. THE PITCH

*(One turn, two breaths, then stop talking. The reason line comes from the lead's class in the CSV, checked before you dial.)*

*(likely fires here → NOT INTERESTED · ALREADY HAVE A WEBSITE · WE'VE GOT OUR FACEBOOK · DON'T NEED A WEBSITE · BURNED BEFORE · NO TIME · HOW MUCH)*

- **"Hey [BUSINESS NAME], my name is Kenzo, and I work with JU.MP Web Design. I saw your business on Google Maps and noticed that [REASON], so I figured I'd give you a quick call to offer our services."**

  - CLASS A, no website or social-only → [REASON] = **"you don't seem to have a website"**
  - CLASS A, dead or broken site → [REASON] = **"your website doesn't seem to be working"**
  - CLASS B, outdated site → [REASON] = **"your website looks like it hasn't been updated in a while"** → CLASS B FORK, before the offer breath

- **"We partner with small businesses in order to run their websites and help create better online branding. We charge $48 a month, and that covers everything you would need to get your website up and running and maintained. Is that something you would be interested in?"**

  *(Stop talking. Let them respond.)*

  INTERESTED / "YEAH, ACTUALLY" / "WE'VE BEEN MEANING TO DO THAT":

  - → 4. THE BOOK, straight in

  CURIOUS / "TELL ME MORE" / "WHAT DOES THE $48 COVER?":

  - **"The $48 covers four main things: your hosting and domain, SSL and security checks, proactive website monitoring, and four edits or revisions every month. So rather than paying someone to build a website once and then having to figure out maintenance yourself, we're managing it for you on an ongoing basis."**
    - Warms up → 4. THE BOOK
    - Asks more → 3. HIS QUESTIONS

  "HOW MUCH?" *(missed the price)*:

  - **"$48 a month, flat, and there's no upfront cost."**
    - → back to the interest question

  NOT THE DECISION MAKER / "YOU'D HAVE TO TALK TO THE OWNER":

  - → GATEKEEPER

  CLASS B FORK *(outdated site only; this replaces the offer breath)*:

  - **"Gotcha. Are you happy with the website you have now?"**
    - Not happy → **"What would you say you don't like about it?"** → *(listen, then the offer breath, aimed at what he named)*
    - Happy with it → **"No worries. If you ever want it redone or want somebody managing it for you, we're around. Have a good one."** → out, NOT INTERESTED

# 3. HIS QUESTIONS

*(His questions, never yours. Answer short, from these anchors, and end every turn one step toward the book. There is no discovery on this call, that's the web designer's meeting.)*

*(likely fires here → WIX IS CHEAPER · WHY MONTHLY · TEMPLATES · E-COMMERCE · CUSTOM REQUESTS · WHO ARE YOU GUYS)*

  "DO I HAVE TO MANAGE THE WEBSITE MYSELF?":

  - **"No. That's one of the main reasons our service exists. We handle the ongoing website management so you don't have to figure everything out yourself."**

  "WHAT IF I NEED CHANGES?":

  - **"You get four edits or revisions each month as part of the $48 package."**

  "DO YOU DO LOGOS?" / "CAN YOU MAKE BUSINESS CARDS?":

  - **"Yes, branding is an important part of what we do. We can help establish your logo, colors, and visual identity, and anything extra like business cards gets designed to match your website."**

  "WHAT MAKES YOU DIFFERENT?":

  - **"One thing that makes us different is that we don't just throw stock images onto a template. We create custom illustrations and branding so the website actually feels like it belongs to your business."**

  "HOW FAST CAN IT BE UP?":

  - **"Usually the designer has a draft to show you within a few business days, and he'll give you the exact timeline on the consultation."** *(Never promise a launch date.)*

  "CAN I SEE YOUR WORK?":

  - **"Yeah. I can send you one of our recent [INDUSTRY] sites right after this call so you can see the style. And on the consultation the designer will actually show you a draft of your own."** *[EXAMPLES: get the example-site links from Joel]*

  "CAN YOU FIX THE SITE I HAVE?" *(Class B)*:

  - **"That's exactly what the consultation is for. He'll look at what you've got, show you what he'd do with it, and make you a draft. You don't pay anything unless you like it."**
    - → 4. THE BOOK

  AFTER ANY ANSWER, THE STEP FORWARD:

  - Warm → 4. THE BOOK
  - Another question → answer it, same law
  - Cooling off → **"Tell you what, the easiest way to see if it's for you is just to look at a draft."** → 4. THE BOOK

# 4. THE BOOK

- **"Tell you what, the easiest next step is a free consultation with our web designer. He'll walk you through what your website would look like, and he'll actually make you a draft of it. If you don't like the draft, no worries, it costs you nothing. If you like it, you can go ahead and get it launched from there. Want me to set that up?"**

  ACCEPTS:

  - → 5. WRAP

  "HOW LONG IS THE CALL?":

  - **"Fifteen, twenty minutes, and he works around your schedule."**
    - → 5. WRAP

  "IS THE CONSULTATION FREE?" / "WHAT'S THE DRAFT COST?":

  - **"Both free. The only thing you'd ever pay is the $48 a month, and only if you like the draft and want the site launched."**
    - → 5. WRAP

  "DO I NEED TO PREPARE ANYTHING?":

  - **"Nothing. He'll ask about your business and what you want, and he takes it from there."**
    - → 5. WRAP

  HESITATES / "LET ME THINK ABOUT IT.":

  - **"Sure. The only thing I'd say is the consultation doesn't commit you to anything. You see the draft first, and you only pay if you actually like it. That's the whole point of it."**
    - Comes around → 5. WRAP
    - Still thinking → **"No problem. When's a good time for me to check back, later this week or next week?"** → CALLBACK SET

  DECLINES BUT WARM:

  - **"All good. If you change your mind, we're at designedbyjump.com, and everything I told you is on there. Have a good one."**
    - → out, NOT INTERESTED

# 5. WRAP *(every booked meeting, every item)*

*(Booking runs on Joel's Calendly, live on the call. He blocks out his busy times; whatever shows open is bookable. The consultation is a phone call from Joel.)*

1. Check the Calendly → **"He's got [DAY] at [TIME], or [DAY] at [TIME]. Which one works better for you?"** → their time
2. **"And what was your name? ...Alright, [NAME]."**
3. **"What's the best number for him to reach you at?"** → read it back
4. **"And what's a good email for the confirmation?"** → read it back letter by letter
5. **"So [NAME], our web designer will give you a call [DAY] at [TIME]. He'll go over what the site would look like and get your draft started. If anything changes on your end, just call or text this number."**
6. Out. No goodbye theater. → MEETING BOOKED

    - Gives a window → **"Alright, I'll try back then. Thank you."** → out, CALLBACK SET, window logged
    - Won't say → **"No worries. Have a good one."** → out, NO ANSWER *(recycle at a different hour)*

  "YOU'D HAVE TO TALK TO THE OWNER" *(mid-call)*:

  - Same window harvest as above.

# DISPOSITIONS *(every call ends in exactly one; the note carries where it died)*

- NO ANSWER: nobody picked up, or it rang to voicemail. Recycle at a different hour in two to three days. Never leave a voicemail.
- VM (RANG OUT): logged only if Teleblast tracks it separately from NO ANSWER; same recycle rule.
- BAD NUMBER: dead line, wrong business, closed. Fix from the lead source or discard.
- CALLBACK SET: a named time or window, from him or the gatekeeper. Kept to the minute. The note says where to resume.
- MEETING BOOKED: consultation set with the web designer, wrap complete, details sent to Joel.
- NOT INTERESTED: a clear no, anywhere on the map. Cooling window, then the rotation.
- NOT A FIT: happy with their current setup, chain or franchise, or genuinely excluded. No redial without a real reason.
- DO NOT CALL: they said the words. Honored instantly and forever.
